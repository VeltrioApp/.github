### What Veltrio brings together

- **Stremio addons** — Cinemeta, community catalogs, 1-click Stremio account import, and automatic watch-history sync on launch.
- **Debrid services** — Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, TorBox, and Premiumize with PIN/device-code sign-in.
- **Personal media servers** — Plex, Jellyfin, and Emby unified on the same title pages with synced resume.
- **Live TV & IPTV** — M3U and Xtream Codes playlists, full EPG guide, instant channel zapping, and catchup replay.
- **Native `libmpv` 4K HDR player** — hardware decoding, surround audio, remembered audio/subtitle preferences, AirPlay, Chromecast, PiP, and offline downloads.
- **10-second TV pairing** — transfer all addons, accounts, IPTV playlists, and Collection from iPhone or Mac to Apple TV / Android TV over encrypted LAN with a 6-digit code.
